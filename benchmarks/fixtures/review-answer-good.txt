Two defects found:

1. Off-by-one bug in cartTotal: the loop uses `i <= items.length`, which reads
   items[items.length] (undefined) on the final iteration and produces NaN. The
   condition must be `i < items.length`.

2. Orphaned @spec annotation: `@spec CART-TOTAL-999` references a spec id that
   does not exist in the known spec ids (the real id is CART-TOTAL-001). This is
   an orphaned forward reference and must be corrected.

Do not ship until both are fixed.
